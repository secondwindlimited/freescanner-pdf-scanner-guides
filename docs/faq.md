# FreeScanner FAQ

## Do I need an account to scan documents?

No. Scanning, cropping, rotating and saving a PDF on your phone all work without signing in. An account is only needed for cloud sync and the web workspace.

## Where are my scans stored?

On your device. If you sign in and turn on sync, eligible documents are also stored privately in the cloud so you can reach them from the web workspace.

## What is never uploaded?

Password-protected PDFs, drafts and OCR text stay on the device, even with sync on.

## What can I do in the web workspace?

Find, rename, reorder, download or delete your synced documents from a browser at [freescanner.secondwindltd.xyz](https://freescanner.secondwindltd.xyz/).

## Which phones does FreeScanner support?

iPhone ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6792426877)) and Android ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.secondwind.freescanner)).

## I can search text inside the app, but not in the exported PDF. Why?

In-app search uses OCR text kept on the device. An exported PDF is only searchable in other apps if it carries its own text layer. See [the full explanation](https://secondwindltd.xyz/guides/search-in-app-not-exported-pdf/), and check any file with the free [PDF text layer checker](https://freescanner.secondwindltd.xyz/en/tools/pdf-text-layer-checker).

Follow the steps at [freescanner.secondwindltd.xyz/delete-account](https://freescanner.secondwindltd.xyz/delete-account).
