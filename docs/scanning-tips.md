# How to scan documents with your phone: 12 tips for sharp, small PDFs

These tips work with any phone scanner app. They are written for [FreeScanner](https://freescanner.secondwindltd.xyz/).

## Before you shoot

1. **Use a dark, plain background** for white paper. Contrast helps the app find the page edges.
2. **Light from the side, not from above.** Overhead light throws your phone's shadow onto the page. A window to one side is ideal.
3. **Flatten the page.** Smooth out folds; hold curled receipts down at the corners, outside the text area.
4. **Clean the lens.** A fingerprint smear is the most common cause of soft scans.

## While you scan

5. **Hold the phone parallel to the page.** Perspective correction can fix an angle, but it costs sharpness.
6. **Fill the frame**, leaving a small margin around the page.
7. **Tap to focus on text**, then hold still for a moment before capturing.
8. **Turn off the flash** on glossy paper, ID cards and laminated pages to avoid glare.

## After you scan

9. **Check the crop** on every page, especially the corners where page numbers live.
10. **Rotate now, not later.** Sideways pages are harder to fix once the PDF is shared.
11. **Put pages in order before export.** See [Organize multi-page scans](https://freescanner.secondwindltd.xyz/guides/organize-multipage-scans).
12. **Name the file for search:** `2026-11-14 electricity bill.pdf` beats `Scan 37.pdf`.

## Specific documents

- **Receipts:** scan the same day, since thermal paper fades. Full walkthrough: [Scan receipts to PDF](https://freescanner.secondwindltd.xyz/guides/scan-receipts-to-pdf).
- **Long documents:** estimate the result first with the [PDF scan size calculator](https://secondwindltd.xyz/tools/pdf-scan-size-calculator/).
- **Anything you need to search later:** confirm the export has a text layer with the [PDF text layer checker](https://freescanner.secondwindltd.xyz/en/tools/pdf-text-layer-checker).
- **Sensitive paperwork:** scan without signing in so the file stays on your phone. See [Scan documents without an account](https://freescanner.secondwindltd.xyz/guides/scan-documents-without-account).
